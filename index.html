Overview

Five countries, one conversation on safer anesthesia

Hosted by the Study Program of Anesthesiology and Intensive Therapy, Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences, Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta, together with SCPA and PERDATIN. Anesthesiologists and intensivists from Indonesia, Malaysia, Iran, Türkiye and Uzbekistan share clinical experience and current evidence across the perioperative journey, from the airway to recovery.

Scientific focus

Geriatric Anesthesia
